**2024 · APSIPA ASC 2024** ·
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/APSIPAASC63619.2025.10848686)

Developed an end-to-end multi-perceptual learning framework that integrates a
Triple-Cross-Fusion GAN for OCT image denoising with a ResNet-based classifier for
retinal disease recognition using PyTorch and OpenCV.

- Conducted controlled comparative experiments under consistent dataset and
  evaluation settings.
- Improved classification accuracy by **more than one percentage point** and
  reconstruction quality while sustaining **real-time GPU inference**.
