[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/APSIPAASC63619.2025.10848686)

This project combines a Triple-Cross-Fusion GAN with multi-perceptual learning for
joint retinal OCT denoising and classification. The method improves image quality
and classification accuracy while retaining practical GPU inference speed.
