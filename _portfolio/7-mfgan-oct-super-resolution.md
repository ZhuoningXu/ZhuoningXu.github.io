**2025 · IWAIT 2025** · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3057230)

MFGAN combines blind-degradation modeling with multi-frame fusion to jointly perform
denoising and super-resolution for retinal OCT images without paired ground-truth
data. Experiments on PKU37 and the VIP Cup 2024 dataset show improvements in visual
quality and quantitative reconstruction performance.
